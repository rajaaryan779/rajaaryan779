# ChaseUp go-to-market (first 30 days)

Confidence tags: [Likely] = strong inference, [Guessing] = untested. Nothing here is validated yet; the plan exists to validate it.

## 1. Who to sell to
- **Ideal customer [Likely]:** solo freelancers and 1-10 person service businesses (designers, developers, consultants, agencies, CAs/accountants' small clients, photographers) who invoice 5+ clients a month and have sent a "gentle reminder" email in the last 90 days.
- **Not yet [Likely]:** anyone needing automatic sending, payment collection or accounting. The MVP does neither.
- **Pain to confirm [Guessing]:** the problem is awkwardness and forgetting, not lack of tools. Ask, don't assume.

## 2. Validate before building more (week 1)
Goal: 10 conversations, 3 people who say they'd use it weekly, 1 who'd pay.
Questions (do not pitch first):
1. Last time a client paid late, what did you do?
2. How do you track who owes you today?
3. What did the last late payment cost you?
4. What have you tried? Why did you stop?
Then show the live app. Pay attention to what they try to click.

## 3. Where to find them (free channels, in order)
1. **Your own network:** ask 20 people "who do you know that freelances or runs a small service business?" Warm intros convert far better than cold. [Likely]
2. **Communities where late payment is a recurring complaint:** r/freelance, r/smallbusiness, indie hacker / designer / developer Slack and Discord groups, local CA and freelancer WhatsApp/Telegram groups. Read the rules; many ban promotion. Help first, mention the tool only when it is relevant.
3. **LinkedIn:** search "freelance" + your city/niche, comment on posts about late payments, then DM. 10 personalised messages a day, not a blast.
4. **Content that attracts the pain:** "Payment reminder email templates (friendly/firm/final)" as a free page. [Likely] search-friendly and it is literally the product's output.
5. **Later, paid:** only after 3 paying users and a known conversion path.

## 4. Message templates (edit; never send unedited)
**DM / email, warm:**
> Hi {name}, I'm building a small tool for freelancers who hate chasing late invoices: it shows who owes what and drafts a polite-to-firm reminder. Could I get 15 minutes of your honest feedback? Not selling anything yet.

**Community post:**
> How do you handle clients who pay late? I'm collecting what actually works (and what feels awkward). Happy to share the reminder templates I end up with.

**Follow-up after 4 days, once:**
> Totally fine if it's not a fit. If useful, here's the template pack regardless: {link}

## 5. Pricing hypothesis [Guessing]
Free up to 5 open invoices; paid tier (test ₹299-499 / $5-9 per month) for unlimited + AI-polished drafts. Test by asking "would you pay X?" and watching hesitation, then by taking money from the first 3.

## 6. Metrics that matter
Conversations held, % who sign up, % who add a real invoice within 24h, % still active in week 2, paying users. Ignore page views.

## 7. Rules I will follow for you
- No fake testimonials, user counts or logos on any page.
- No scraping emails or bulk cold outreach; consent and platform rules matter, and spam burns your domain.
- I draft, you send.
